# Ninkear N4 Mini PC Quiet Fan Enclosure

## 🤫 The Backstory: When Silence Becomes an Engineering Challenge

Ever tried working next to a mini PC that sounds like a jet engine taking off? Meet my solution - a custom 3D-printable shell designed to replace the ear-splitting fan of the Ninkear N4 with a whisper-quiet Noctua alternative.

## 🛠 Project Motivation

After countless meetings where my colleagues thought I was running a small aircraft maintenance facility in my home office, I decided: enough is enough. This project was born out of pure, unadulterated frustration with the stock fan's noise levels.

## 📐 Design Highlights

- **Dimensions**: 114mm x 106mm x 38mm shell
- **Fan Compatibility**: 80mm 5V PWM Noctua fan
- **Wall Thickness**: 1.5mm
- **Mounting**: Integrated screw pillars and corner extensions for stability

## 🖨 3D Printing Notes

- Recommended print material: PLA or PETG
- Supports not likely needed due to design
- Suggested infill: 20-25%

## 🔧 Customization Potential

Feel free to tweak dimensions in the `mini_pc_shell_cq.py` script. CadQuery makes modifications relatively straightforward!

## 🚨 Disclaimer

- First-time 3D design project
- Measure twice, print once
- Your mileage may vary
